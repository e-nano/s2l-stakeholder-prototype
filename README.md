# S2L stakeholder prototype

Open `index.html` in any modern browser. No installation, build step, server, Agrii-system connection or internet connection is required.

## v0.9.4 demonstration route

1. Start as **Alex Morgan · all non-lab actions**. The home screen shows one permission-driven workspace rather than separate Sales, Operations, Operator and Finance dashboards.
2. Open **Users, hierarchy & permissions**. Review the three-director, five-manager and twelve-operator demonstration hierarchy. Rename Sarah's display title to Master and confirm that no access changes. Move her between the North and South director branches to recompute scope without copying permissions or history. Then grant her the planning action independently.
3. Switch the facilitator-only **Demo actor** selector to Sarah. Planning now appears beside her existing field work because of the new action and branch scope. Remove the grant from Alex's Master screen to show it disappearing again.
4. Return to Alex and open **Customers & commercial**. View Emma's and Jacob's opportunities, then try the collaboration/transfer route. Shared visibility does not grant editing or ownership takeover.
5. Open **Green Estate** and review the S2L-WEB-003 CRM tracker. Switch between timeline and table views, distinguish an actual interaction from a no-answer attempt, planned follow-up and automatic system event, then log an earlier interaction on behalf of Sarah and Emma. Confirm that occurrence time, actual participants, recorder, purpose, outcome, linked work and follow-up remain separate.
6. Open **Areas & orders**, then prepare a new order. Select the whole North Field, a child or deeper Area. Save Upper Section – West as a draft, review it with the publish action, return to the preserved order and select it explicitly.
7. Open **Operations** and assign JOB-145 to Alex. This is a valid self-assignment because Alex has planning, assignment and sampling actions; assignment is still separate from permission and competence.
8. Open **Field work**. The same user can access the work pack, record notes and synchronise without switching to an Operator account. The note review shows the structured customer/contact, participant, occurrence, purpose, recorder and linked-work fields before a CRM interaction is confirmed. Smart Case identity, weighing, GNSS and offline rules remain unchanged.
9. Open **Shipments & returns**. Demonstrate that Alex's broad permissions cannot bypass incomplete sampling, missing synchronisation or unresolved reconciliation. Then progress the synthetic outward journey.
10. Switch to **Maya Patel · laboratory actions** for checked receipt, report upload/publication and eligible liner-free pod return. Laboratory actions remain separately authorised from Alex's all-non-lab access.
11. Return to Alex for published-report access, commercial disposition and individual returned-pod reconciliation. Carrier delivery, checked receipt and reusable-pod readiness remain separate states.
12. Switch to **Tom Green · customer account** to demonstrate self-service order/support and published-report access. Customer-originated commercial and support notifications are web/in-app only.
13. Open Alex's **Notification settings**. Toggle applicable process steps and show that preferences do not grant record access, remove responsibility, hide history or change workflow readiness.

The prototype follows the v0.9.4 action-based direction and its Action Catalogue and User Hierarchy companion documents, including section 4.12.1 and accepted requirement S2L-WEB-003. All participants are users; optional titles are descriptive only; Master configures hierarchy, actions and scopes; views follow effective permissions; one user may perform all non-laboratory work; laboratory work remains separately authorised. The first deployment is standalone S2L. All data and external outcomes are fictional simulations, not acceptance evidence.
